### Hej there, I'm Niklas 👋

I’m a **Geologist** working on safer communities, based in Denmark 🇩🇰. I analyse natural hazards and physical climate risks, from floods to storms, and communicate them so people can act. 

#### 🏢 What I'm building

- **[geofare →](https://geofare.dk/)** – my independent climate risk consultancy: physical climate risk assessment, spatial analysis and risk communication, with tools for emergency planners and climate adaptation teams.
- **[FloodReady.dk →](https://floodready.dk/)** – a free flood risk check for every Danish home (stormflod, skybrud, grundvand, vandløb), plus advice on how to adapt. Launching soon.
- By day, I support users of EUMETSAT's data services (Data Store, EUMDAC, EUMETView) via Innoflair 🛰️

#### 🌱 Earlier & ongoing

- With **[OpenSpaceData →](https://www.openspacedata.org/)** I founded an organisation on a mission to democratise satellite data through easy-to-use tools and education.
- With **[DisasterTech →](https://www.disaster-tech.org/)** I co-founded and led an international community of designers, developers, scientists and engineers building tools for people in crisis.

#### 📣 What I believe

Better (flood) risk models alone won't save anyone: **the last mile matters more than the last decimal.** How risk is communicated decides whether people act. Read more in [*Stop Building Better Flood Models*]([https://magazine.floodindustry.com/books/Issue-13-JulyAug2026/](https://risk-and-haz.offprint.app/a/3mozncdvl7k23-stop-building-better-flood-models-why-the-last-mile-matters-more-than-the-last-decimal).
